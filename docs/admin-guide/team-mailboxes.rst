Team mailboxes
==============

A team mailbox is a mailbox several accounts share: a message to its address
lands once, in a mailbox each member opens, unlike a mailing list, which
copies it to every member.

When the deployment keeps them apart from the lists, the
:guilabel:`Team mailboxes` entry of the sidebar lists them, and they are
created there rather than among the groups.

Creating a team mailbox
-----------------------

#. In the team mailboxes list, click :guilabel:`New team mailbox`.
#. Fill in the form (see :ref:`filling-in-a-form`):

   - :guilabel:`Name` \*, which cannot be changed afterwards;
   - :guilabel:`Email` \*: letters, digits, ``#``, ``-`` and ``_`` before the
     ``@``, in an allowed domain (see :ref:`email-address`);
   - :guilabel:`Members` \*: at least one, found by their address. They must
     have their address at the team mailbox's domain;
   - :guilabel:`Owners`, :guilabel:`Description`, :guilabel:`Department`
     as for a group.

#. Click :guilabel:`Create`.

Twake Mail creates the mailbox and gives its members access to it. Adding or
removing a member later changes who opens it.
